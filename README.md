## Product Philosophy

I care about:
- **Starting with the problem**, not the feature
- Using data and customer behaviour to find meaningful opportunities
- Designing simple experiences for complex problems
- Running structured experiments and learning quickly
- Partnering deeply with engineering and design
- Measuring products by **customer outcomes and business impact**
