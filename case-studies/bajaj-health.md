# Bajaj Finserv Health — Selected Product Work

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Domain:** HealthTech · Activation · Conversion · Trust · Automation

## Scope

Owned product roadmaps across diagnostics, consultations, wellness, insurance, and internal operations—from discovery and strategy through launch, GTM, and iteration.

## Smart Health Reports — Turning passive reports into informed next steps

Health Files allowed customers to upload lab reports, but adoption was low because the product did not create enough value after upload. Customers told us they were left to interpret unfamiliar parameters themselves.

We built Smart Health Reports to normalise lab data across formats and present parameters in plain language with historical trends. When an out-of-range result indicated a relevant service, the product offered an **optional next step**—for example, a diabetes consultation—rather than leaving customers to navigate the issue alone.

**Outcome:** Active Health Files users increased **8×**, showing that a useful report experience drove sustained adoption.

The product principle was to replace fragmented, anxious searching with understandable context and a customer-controlled route to care.

## Doctor discovery — Ranking for patient value and trust

Doctor listings had no meaningful ranking logic. We designed a two-layer model:
- **Preference score:** experience, patient ratings, and a fair-fees index
- **Trust gate:** attendance and profile-quality signals that could reduce visibility when reliability was weak

The weighting was treated as a product hypothesis to validate through live behaviour, not as permanent truth. Gender was deliberately excluded from the algorithmic score and instead surfaced as a patient-controlled contextual filter where relevant.

**Outcome:** **18% CTR improvement** and **11.6% annual revenue lift** after the listing reorder.
