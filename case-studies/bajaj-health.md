# Bajaj Finserv Health — Selected Product Work

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Domain:** HealthTech · Wellness · Diagnostics · Consultations · Insurance

## Scope

Owned product roadmaps across diagnostics, consultations, wellness, insurance, and internal operations, working from discovery and strategy through launch, GTM, and scale.

## Product approach

My work involved identifying customer and operational problems, translating them into product opportunities, and partnering with engineering, design, and business teams to build and launch solutions.

## Selected outcomes

### Diabetes wellness — 0→1
Built a diabetes wellness business from concept through strategy, GTM, and launch while leading a 4-person APM team.

**Outcome:** ₹28L revenue in five months.

### Smart Health Reports
Turned static diagnostic reports into a more useful health-insight and cross-selling experience.

**Outcome:** ₹7L+ incremental MRR.

### Doctor discovery
Built a doctor quality score using signals including cancellations, ratings, profile completeness, and fee fairness to improve search ordering.

**Outcome:** 18% CTR improvement and 11.6% annual revenue growth.

### Insurance automation
Replaced a manual insurance pre-authorisation bottleneck with OCR and named-entity recognition based automation.

**Outcome:** 26% higher processing throughput and ₹17L annual savings.

### ABHA acquisition
Built an acquisition and onboarding journey around ABHA integration.

**Outcome:** 15K+ users and 44K+ Health ID registrations in four months.

## Product takeaway

Across healthcare products, a recurring theme was converting complex operational or clinical workflows into simpler digital experiences without losing the underlying business constraints.
