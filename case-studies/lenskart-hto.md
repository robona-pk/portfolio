# Lenskart@Home — Product Case Study

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Domain:** O2O Commerce · Eyewear · Growth · CX

## Context

Lenskart@Home (HTO) connects customers with an at-home eyewear trial experience. My product scope focused on **growth, conversion, customer experience, and retention** across the customer journey.

## The product problem

The HTO journey had multiple points where customer intent could be lost — from uncertainty during booking to friction in the journey and post-purchase issues.

Rather than treating these as isolated feature requests, I used funnel behaviour and customer signals to identify where product intervention could recover demand or improve the experience.

## Product approach

I worked across:

- Funnel and behavioural analysis
- Problem discovery and prioritisation
- Product requirements and solution design
- Experimentation
- Engineering and cross-functional execution
- Measurement of customer and business outcomes

## Selected outcomes

- Contributed **₹6Cr+ in incremental annualized revenue** across HTO initiatives
- Improved the booking funnel through structured experimentation
- Recovered otherwise-lost demand by connecting digital intent with offline store journeys
- Improved post-purchase retention through a WhatsApp customer journey for exchanges and repairs

## What I learned

The strongest HTO opportunities were often not about adding more features. They came from understanding **where customer intent was breaking down**, then designing the smallest product intervention that could reconnect that intent to a successful outcome.
