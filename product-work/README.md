# Independent AI Products & Experiments

Products and prototypes I have built to explore product ideas hands-on. Mira and Charlie are featured in the **Independent AI Products** section of the portfolio site.

## Mira

**Evidence-grounded portfolio assistant · Live**

A production assistant on this portfolio that answers from approved professional evidence. Gemini selects supporting sources, while deterministic guardrails, output validation and an evidence-bound cache keep unsupported claims out. It is backed by a 341-case evaluation harness and a 13-question blind production audit, which led to eight new regression cases.

## Charlie the Voice Booking Agent

**Voice AI product experiment · 2026**

A phone-style booking assistant for at-home eye tests and frame trials, designed for customers who are less comfortable with apps. A deterministic state machine handles serviceability checks, slot selection and confirmation.

## What these builds demonstrate

I use prototypes to go beyond writing a PRD:

- Define a user problem and hypothesis
- Translate product thinking into an executable MVP
- Make trade-offs around scope and architecture
- Test interaction patterns quickly
- Explore AI capabilities and limitations, with evaluations to measure them
- Iterate based on what the product actually does
